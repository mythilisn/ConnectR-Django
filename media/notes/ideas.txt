Get Started Flow - Wireframe

1. Get Started Button Click

User clicks on the 'Get Started' button.

Redirect to an Onboarding Modal or Full-Screen Page.

2. Interactive Onboarding Steps

Step 1: Select Interests

Display options like: Mathematics, Programming, History, Science, Business, etc.

Multi-select enabled.

Step 2: Choose Document Types

Options include: Notes, Presentations, PDFs, Flashcards.

User can prioritize types (e.g., prefer Notes over Presentations).

Step 3: Learning Goals

Options include: Exam Prep, Skill Building, Quick Revision, Research.

Helps in curating personalized recommendations.

Button: Continue ➡️ leads to Explore Mode.

3. Explore Mode (No Login Required)

Display a dashboard with:

Featured Documents (Top-rated or trending in their chosen categories).

'Recently Added' section with fresh uploads.

'Popular in Your Interests'—based on what they selected earlier.

Call to Action: 'Sign Up to Access Full Content & Uploads.'

Sections:

Top Collaborators: Highlighting active members in the community.

Community Picks: Highly recommended notes by peers.

4. Call to Action

When attempting to view full content, a modal pops up encouraging Sign Up or Login.

Smooth transition back to Explore Mode after signup.

5. Future Enhancements (Suggestions)

AI-based recommendations based on browsing history.

Option to join study groups directly from the Explore Mode.

Flashcard generation from uploaded notes for quick revision.
